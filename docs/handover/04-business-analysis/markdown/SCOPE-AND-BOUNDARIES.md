## In scope (implemented)

- E01 Site navigation and global chrome
- E02 Page heroes and page headers
- E03 Editorial content presentation
- E04 Media

- E06 Forms and lead capture
- E07 Conversational experience

- E09 Measurement, accessibility and performance

## Out of scope (not implemented)

- Adobe Tags, Web SDK, Analytics, CJA, Target, consent platform
- Forms submission back end
- Personalization
- CDN redirects (on hold)
- A separate AEM Assets environment (KB-D-064 not adopted)
